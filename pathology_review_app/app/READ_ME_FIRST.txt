HRS CELL REVIEW -- quick start
==============================

You have two files:

  reviewer.html        the review app (opens in your web browser; nothing to install)
  P3HRS-....zip        the images -- do NOT unzip it

1. Put both files in one folder on your computer (for example Documents/HRS_review).
2. Double-click reviewer.html. It opens in your browser. Chrome or Edge recommended;
   Firefox works. Use the SAME browser every session.
3. Type your initials, press "Open data bundle (.zip)..." and choose the .zip.
4. For each cell (marked by a crosshair): answer the checklist (Y/N/U or 1/2/3), give a
   1-5 score, add notes if you like, and press Enter (or "Save & next"). The arrow keys
   move down the whole form. Press ? at any time for help and keyboard shortcuts.
5. Answers are saved in the browser as you go. To continue another day, open
   reviewer.html again: in Chrome/Edge press "Continue with ..." (one click to allow
   access); otherwise choose the same .zip. You resume where you stopped.
6. At the end of each session press "Export answers". A CSV named
   HRS_review_<initials>_<bundle>_<date-time>.csv lands in your Downloads folder.
   Email the newest one back. Exporting again later is fine; each file contains everything.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the app runs entirely on your computer.

If your Mac unzipped the .zip automatically when it downloaded, use
"Open an unzipped bundle folder..." and choose that folder instead.
